Idle
Same safe steps as the tone slider: each step up is limited to 2 dB and Page Up/Down, Home and End are disabled.

Network stream

The board listens on TCP port 4010. From a PC run python tools/stream.py 192.168.2.40 song.mp3, or add --device "CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)" to send system audio. The password is asked on the command line.

Clip manager

Drop .mp3 or .wav files here, or
MP3 (any bit rate) and 16-bit WAV play; other sample rates are converted. Names: letters, digits, dot, dash, underscore. Mono 96 kbps MP3 is about 12 KB per second, so the 12 MB holds roughly 15 minutes. Do not upload or delete while something is playing (flash writes can glitch the sound).

Log in

Stays logged in on this browser for 30 days. The page uses plain HTTP, so use it on your own network only.

Test tone

Safe sliders: each step up is limited to 2 dB, so clicking the track or dragging fast cannot jump to a loud level. Page Up/Down, Home and End are disabled. Press Esc to stop. The tone also stops by itself within 15 seconds if this page is closed or loses the connection.

Ambient scene

A background loop (rain, a fan) plays underneath, and each "random event" clip plays at a random moment between its two times. The scene follows the amp volume and the speaker limit, uses the EQ, and pauses by itself while you play a clip, a stream or the test tone. The level is relative to the amp volume (0 dB = as loud as that, lower = quieter).

Network audio from Windows (VBAN and Scream)

Voicemeeter (VBAN): open the VBAN window, add an outgoing stream, set this board's address and port 6980, pick a stream name, switch it on. Scream: note that the official Scream driver cannot be installed normally on Windows 11 (its signature expired), see docs/windows-setup.md; with a working driver, choose "Speakers (Scream)" and send to this board's address (unicast) or leave multicast on. Easier on Windows 11: a virtual audio cable plus tools/stream.py (docs/windows-setup.md). Neither protocol has a password, so use the address filter. Sound goes through the EQ and the speaker-profile volume cap, and a clip or stream you start here wins over them.

Internet radio

MP3 streams over http:// or https:// work. AAC, Ogg and HLS stations do not (the board says "not MP3"). Station names up to 23 characters, addresses without spaces.

OSC synthesizer

Test keys play C4 to C5 for half a second (the amp volume and the speaker limit apply, and notes below the speaker's lowest frequency are ignored). OSC addresses: /synth/note note vel, /synth/noteoff note, /synth/alloff, /synth/freq hz [vel], /synth/wave 0-3|sine|tri|saw|square, /synth/adsr a d s r, /synth/cutoff hz, /synth/volume 0-1. Changes sent over OSC are live only; Save stores the page's values.

Speaker EQ

The same filters run on both channels, for tones, streams and clips. The preamp is automatic: it cancels the largest boost, so the EQ never raises the level above the loudness cap. Limits: 20–20000 Hz, Q 0.2–10, gain −15 to +6 dB. Untick "EQ enabled" and press Apply to compare with the bare sound.

Speaker profile and limits

Streaming delay

How much sound the Brick collects before it starts playing a stream or clip. Less = lower delay, but more risk of dropouts if the network hiccups. Wired Ethernet copes with the low setting; choose Normal or Safe if you hear dropouts.

Amp power saving

When nothing has played for a while the amp output goes Hi-Z, and later the amp chip powers down completely. Any sound wakes it again with no audible delay. 0 = never.

Firmware update

The board keeps the previous image and rolls back if the new one does not come online.
